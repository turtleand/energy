Lesson 1 Lab: Energy flow in a simple circuit

Adjust voltage, resistance, time, and loop state to see current, power, and transferred energy change together.

This artifact turns the first circuit model into something visible. It keeps the physics simple on purpose: a battery, a loop, resistance, a useful load, and meters for the relationships.

Simple circuit model

Push, flow, work, loop.

Closed loop. Current is flowing.

Current 0.25 A

Current is flow. It rises with voltage and falls with resistance.

Power 1.25 W

Power is work per second. It depends on both push and flow.

Energy transferred 12.5 J

Energy grows as power continues over time.

The relationships

  • Current = voltage divided by resistance.
  • Power = voltage multiplied by current.
  • Energy = power multiplied by time.
  • If the loop opens, sustained current stops.
